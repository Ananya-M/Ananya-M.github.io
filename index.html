About

I build signal-processing pipelines and software for bioelectrical sensor data — currently EEG recorded alongside Low-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (LIFU) neuromodulation at UCSF, where I'm researching non-invasive treatment for Alcohol Use Disorder. Before that: a custom PPG sensor board at Cala Health, TMS-evoked potential analysis at Northeastern, and two years in medical-device regulatory compliance.

I care about turning research prototypes into pipelines other people can actually run — modular, documented, and reproducible. My Northstar: infinite learning.

M.S. Bioengineering Northeastern University 2023

Now in progress

A denoising & peak-detection pipeline for evoked potentials

Building a frequency-domain Wiener filter to denoise EEG-derived somatosensory evoked potentials, plus a domain-agnostic peak-detection algorithm to independently localize the N20 and P35 components — without collapsing them into one shared-parameter fit, so treatment effects specific to each neural generator stay visible. Architected as decoupled modules: filter estimation, application, diagnostics, visualization.

  • Wiener filtering
  • Automated feature detection
  • MATLAB / EEGLAB
  • Biosignal classification
