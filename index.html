About

I enjoy building signal-processing pipelines for bioelectrical sensor data. I'm currently working on EEG recorded alongside Low-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (LIFU) neuromodulation in collaboration with the TN2 lab at UCSF. The goal of TN2 lab is to develop cutting-edge non-invasive treatment modalities for Alcohol Use Disorder. Prior to all the EEG noise, I worked developing a custom PPG sensor board at Cala Health; was part of the TMS-EEG research at Northeastern University and worked on medical device regulatory compliance at Panacea Medical Technologies.

Now in progress

A denoising & peak-detection pipeline for evoked potentials

Wrapping up data analysis of a FUS-EEG study with UCSF, mostly focusing on time-domain aspects like RMS and peak to peak measures. Exploring time-frequency analysis, using adaptive filters like Wiener,LMS and RLS filters to denoise EEG-derived somatosensory evoked potentials. Experimenting with LMMs to model the effects of LIFU on EEG responses. Interested in exploring the effects of LIFU on HRV.

  • Automated feature detection
